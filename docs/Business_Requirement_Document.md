# Business Requirement Document

## Project Name

Sosha E-Commerce ERP CRM Platform


## 1. Project Overview

Platform digital yang menggabungkan:

- Online Store
- ERP Management
- CRM
- Android Mobile Application
- Marketplace Integration
- WhatsApp Customer Communication
- Business Intelligence Dashboard


## 2. Business Objective

Tujuan:

1. Membuat channel penjualan online sendiri
2. Mengelola customer secara terpusat
3. Mengelola inventory dan order
4. Monitoring KPI bisnis
5. Integrasi marketplace


## 3. User Roles


### Customer

Fungsi:

- Register/Login
- Melihat produk
- Membeli produk
- Tracking order
- Mendapat notifikasi


### Admin

Fungsi:

- Manage product
- Manage order
- Manage customer
- Manage inventory


### Manager

Fungsi:

- Melihat KPI
- Sales monitoring
- Business report


### Warehouse

Fungsi:

- Stock management
- Packing
- Shipment update
